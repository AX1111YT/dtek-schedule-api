# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of DTEK Schedule API, pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to conduct that supports an open, respectful, and constructive project community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect for differing opinions and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback professionally.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what benefits the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, sexualized language or advances, and unwanted attention.
- Trolling, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and repositories. It also applies when someone represents the project in public or at an event.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit or remove contributions that violate this Code and may take action proportionate to the behavior, including a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent ban.

Report unacceptable behavior to [AX1111YT@proton.me](mailto:AX1111YT@proton.me). Include relevant links, dates, and context. Please do not report security vulnerabilities through this channel; use the process in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Reports will be reviewed fairly, and the privacy of the reporter will be respected.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will consider the impact and severity of each incident when deciding how to respond:

1. **Correction:** For unprofessional or unwelcome conduct, a private warning explaining the issue and expected behavior.
2. **Warning:** For a more serious or repeated violation, a warning with stated consequences for further conduct.
3. **Temporary restriction:** For a serious or sustained violation, a temporary ban from project spaces and contact with affected people.
4. **Permanent ban:** For a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression toward individuals or groups, a permanent ban from project spaces.
